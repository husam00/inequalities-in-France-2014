Objectives
Main Objectives:  	“Compare inequalities in France “
Our primary project objectives are as follows:
 
1. Comprehensive Data Exploration: We aim to thoroughly explore, analyze, and visualize the provided dataset related to the French industry.
This includes uncovering insights about various factors, such as population, salaries, and businesses,
to better understand the dynamics of different regions in France.

2. Data Pre-processing: We plan to clean and preprocess the data to ensure its quality and reliability.
This will involve addressing missing values, handling outliers, and preparing the dataset for advanced analysis.

3. Data Visualization: Our goal is to create meaningful visualizations using libraries like Matplotlib and Seaborn. 
These visualizations will enhance our understanding of the data and assist in answering relevant questions about the dataset.

4. Analysis and Insights: Through statistical analysis, we aim to derive valuable insights from the dataset.
These insights will inform our subsequent modeling and problem-solving efforts.

5. Modeling and testing: our objective to create and define a model where it can predict average salary for man or women.
Where we will compare 3 models and explain why would we choose one than the other, as well how it can be improved. 

Individual Expertise: Each member of our group brings a unique set of skills and expertise to the project. 
While we have varying levels of proficiency in data analysis and machine learning, we are committed to collaborating effectively to achieve our objectives.
Collaboration with Business Experts: As this project is part of our training program, we haven't engaged with external business experts. 
However, we actively consult with our instructors and mentors who provide valuable guidance and expertise in data analytics.
Similar Projects and Their Influence: We are aware that similar projects may exist within our academic and professional circles.
These projects have helped us gain insights into the challenges and opportunities that we might encounter in our analysis.
Our project aims to contribute to this ongoing conversation by providing a new perspective on analyzing French industry data.
